About OnePak

We partner with consumers and businesses to reclaim and recycle old electronics responsibly, helping reduce e-waste and supporting a circular economy.

How It Works

1. Send Your Device

Use our prepaid shipping kit to safely send in your electronics.

2. Evaluation

We assess the device’s condition and determine resale or recycling path.

3. Reuse or Recycle

Devices are refurbished for resale, or responsibly recycled for parts and materials.

4. Make an Impact

Track how your device helps reduce e-waste and supports sustainability.
